Lee Hyoseok

I am currently an AI Researcher on the VARCO 3D Team at NC AI (Research Personnel, Alternative Military Service). I received my master's degree in Artificial Intelligence from POSTECH, advised by Tae-Hyun Oh, and my bachelor's degree from Kookmin University, advised by Junho Kim.

I work on research problems in computer graphics, vision, and machine learning. My research interests include 3D Reconstruction, Computational Photography, and Generative Model, but not limited to.
